FAQ

Common questions

Which payment methods are supported?

Cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad and Visa/Mastercard cards. Turn on only the ones you want.

Do I get mobile apps too?

Yes. Android and iOS apps run alongside the website, on the same products, prices and stock.

Can I add hundreds of products at once?

Yes. Products can be imported in bulk from a CSV file, and edited one by one in the admin portal.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Staff get their own logins with access to the admin work they do, separate from the owner's.

Will it carry my brand?

Your shop name and logo appear across the website, the apps and every invoice.
