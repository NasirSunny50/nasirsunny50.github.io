How it works

From your website to a working assistant.

  1. Point it at your site

    CraftBot reads your sitemap and every page's visible text.

  2. It learns the whole site

    Pages go into a searchable knowledge base, plus a site digest of every product, partner and client.

  3. Paste one script tag

    The chat widget appears on your site in your colours, with your greeting.

  4. It stays in sync

    Nightly re-crawls, a CMS webhook and a "Re-crawl now" button keep answers current.

Install

One tag. Any website.

Works on WordPress, custom sites and any CMS. Colours and greeting can also be set from the admin portal later.

  • No change to your site's code beyond the tag
  • CMS webhook to re-sync when you publish
index.html
<!-- CraftBot chat widget -->
<script
  data-embed-id="your-project-id"
  data-base-api-url="https://bot.yourcompany.com/api/embed"
  data-assistant-name="Website Assistant"
  data-button-color="#0B6574"
  data-greeting="Hi! Ask me anything."
  src="https://bot.yourcompany.com/embed/craftbot.js">
</script>
FAQ

Common questions

Which AI models does it use?

Your choice, per website: Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Ollama for local models, and many more. You can set several in order, so one takes over if another fails.

What happens when we update our website?

CraftBot re-crawls every night and only re-learns pages that changed. You can also trigger a sync from your CMS with a webhook, or press "Re-crawl now" in the admin portal.

Can visitors ask in Bangla?

Yes. Questions in Bangla and other languages are matched against your English pages, and the answer comes back in the visitor's language.

Will it make things up?

It answers from your website's content and a set of key facts you control. You decide what it says when it doesn't know, so it can point visitors to your contact page instead of guessing.
