How it works

Your cameras, made smarter.

  1. Connect your recorder

    Cameriqo reads the camera streams from your existing DVR or NVR. No new cameras.

  2. Enrol your people

    Name, flat, mobile and a face photo: drag it in, pick a file or paste it.

  3. Watch it recognise

    Names appear over faces on the live view; unfamiliar people get a red ID.

  4. Correct it, and it learns

    Confirm or fix a match in one tap. Every correction makes the next one better.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need new cameras?

No. Cameriqo reads the streams from your existing Hikvision-style DVR or NVR. For the best results, one camera near the entrance at face height, in full 1080p, with a white light at night.

What computer does it need?

A small Windows PC next to your recorder. Any DirectX 12 graphics card speeds it up (AMD, Intel or NVIDIA); without one it runs on the processor.

Can I check it from my phone?

Yes. It opens in any browser, and secure remote access lets you check it from outside, behind a login with lockout after repeated wrong tries.

How accurate is it?

It depends most on the camera: face height, resolution and night lighting matter more than any setting. When it isn't sure, it says "not sure" and asks you to review, rather than guessing a name. Your corrections improve it over time.

What about residents' consent?

Only the people you enrol are named. Faces stay on your premises, images are deleted on the schedule you set, and a person can be removed with all their images at any time.
