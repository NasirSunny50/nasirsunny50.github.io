Common questions

Will our existing fingerprint machine work?

ZKTeco and terminals that can send data over the network work directly. Send us the model number and we'll confirm before you pay.

Does it do payroll?

Basic payroll is included: salary structures, monthly runs and payslips. If you need complex tax rules or bank files, we'll look at your case in the demo.

What if the internet goes down?

Terminals keep punches in their own memory and the system collects them once the connection is back.

What if we grow?

Pay only the difference between the two setup fees to move up. Your data stays as it is.

If we stop support, does the software stop?

No. It keeps running. You just stop receiving updates and fixes.
